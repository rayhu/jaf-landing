## How to Apply {#apply}

To apply for any of the positions above, please send your resume and a brief cover letter to [careers@justiceaccessfoundation.org](mailto:careers@justiceaccessfoundation.org). Please indicate the position you are applying for in the subject line.

Justice Access Foundation is an equal opportunity organization. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
